Suito Kurawanka Fireworks Festival 20264,877 fireworks and a drone show

Hello, I'm Robu.

I watched the fifth Suito Kurawanka Fireworks Festival from the Takatsuki venue at Yodogawa River Park on September 20, 2026. Here is a look back at the evening, from the drones lighting up the sky to the fireworks that followed, through photos and videos I took at the event.

About the fifth Suito Kurawanka Fireworks Festival

DateSunday, September 20, 2026
VenuesHirakata and Takatsuki areas of Yodogawa River Park
Fireworks and drone showFrom 19:10
Fireworks4,877, with shells up to size No. 8

The overall event ran from 15:00 to 20:00. The number of fireworks has a special meaning: 4,777 represented the children born in Hirakata, Takatsuki and Katano during the previous year, with another 100 fired in remembrance, making 4,877 in total. I found it moving that a year in the life of the local community was reflected in that number.

Drones painting the night sky

I was watching from the Takatsuki venue. Before the fireworks, around 1,300 drones and the voices of the audience came together for the Oronamin C-sponsored “Genki Hatsuratsu! Ozora Daigassho 2026,” a mass singalong under the open sky.

Fans in hand, the audience sang “Ai wa Katsu” together

We were given handheld fans and invited to join in singing KAN's “Ai wa Katsu,” following the lyrics and musical notation displayed in the sky. The first video, about 20 seconds long, captures a little of the singalong atmosphere at the Takatsuki venue.

Oronamin C's message in the sky

The drones also spelled out the Oronamin C name and “Genki Hatsuratsu!” as fireworks burst beside them. Seeing the sponsor's message presented on the scale of the night sky made for a memorable scene.

This is a record of a sponsored performance I filmed at the event, not a paid promotional feature commissioned for this blog.

Golden fireworks filling the view

The fireworks filled such a wide expanse of the dark sky that even the still images convey their impact. Watching layer upon layer of light unfold overhead was particularly striking in person.

Things to know for a future visit

  • There is no parking at the venue. The organisers advise visitors to use public transport.
  • The Hirakata venue is normally about a 15-minute walk from Keihan Hirakata-shi Station, but after 17:00, crowds may make the journey take around an hour.
  • Reserved seating and event arrangements can change from year to year. Always check the latest official information before your next visit.

Looking back

Suito Kurawanka was more than an impressive fireworks display. The drone show and the meaning the event holds for the local community made it a special evening. Knowing why there were 4,877 fireworks added another dimension to the view. With my own footage at the heart of this report, I have tried to preserve the atmosphere as I experienced it.
