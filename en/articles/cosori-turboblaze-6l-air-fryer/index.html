Cold Fried Food and Side Dishes Come Back to the Table

One of the 6L's features is the official expression "95% oil cut." The idea is not to submerge food in frying oil but to cook with hot air, using less oil while aiming for a crispy outside and a juicy inside. Results vary with the food, the amount, and the settings, but it's an appealing idea for households that like fried food.

There are two easy entry points.

One is air-fried chicken. The official recipe example is 195°C for 15 minutes. There's no pot of oil to set up. You put the chicken in the basket, set the machine, and cooking begins.

The other is the Reheat mode. Use it for yesterday's fried food or a store-bought side dish that has sat for a while. It's an option for times when the microwave alone isn't quite enough. In households where fried food and side dishes tend to be left over, it widens what ends up on the table.

From Meat Dishes and Breakfast to Sweets

Grill and Roast suit the main dish at dinner. The official recipe examples are 230°C for 8 minutes for steak, and 200°C for 15 minutes followed by 175°C for 5 minutes for roast pork. You can also grill chicken and summer vegetables together. You can make these in a frying pan too, but putting them in the basket and leaving the machine to it means less time standing at the stove.

For breakfast, a one-plate meal of toast, eggs, and sausages cooked together is introduced in the official materials. One run puts the whole breakfast together, which is welcome on a rushed morning.

Bake mode handles cheese frittata, muffins, and cake. It suits weekend snacks and baking with kids. A recipe book comes with the machine, so you won't be stuck finding your first dish.

Fermenting and Drying Widen the Fun

The 6L lets you set temperatures from 30°C, so low-temperature cooking is within reach.

For Ferment mode, the official examples include yogurt, amazake, shio-koji, and fermented butter. The official recipe example for fermented butter is 43°C for 9 hours. It takes time, but once it's set, it progresses in the background while you go about your day.

Dry mode lets you make dried fruit and beef jerky. The recipe example for jerky is around 75°C for 4 hours. Snacks and pantry staples you make yourself add a new pleasure to the kitchen.

Keep Warm helps in daily life too. In a household where mealtimes drift apart because of after-school activities or work, you can hold a plate warm for whoever comes home late.

How the DC Motor Changes Waiting Time and Housework

The 6L has a DC motor. It circulates hot air efficiently, and compared with COSORI's 3.5L model, it cuts cooking time by up to 38%. Less time spent waiting for dinner matters because it happens every day.

Fan speed is controlled in five steps, temperature runs from 30 to 230°C, and the timer goes up to 24 hours. From fried chicken that wants strong airflow to fermenting and drying that call for low, steady temperatures, one machine covers it. Once it's set, you aren't stuck at the stove and can move on to laundry or tidying up.

Operating noise is rated at 55 dB or less. Japan's Ministry of the Environment gives 50 dB as the level of a quiet office and 60 dB as a quiet passenger car or normal conversation, so 55 dB sits roughly between them. The motor is quieter than a conventional AC motor and is less likely to compete with family conversation during dinner prep.

Space, Power, and Care

¥19,980 is not a casual purchase, so let's check in advance the points that tend to cause trouble after delivery.

Space. It measures 40.0 cm deep × 30.0 cm wide × 30.1 cm high with the handle, or 34.0 cm deep without it, and weighs 5.2 kg. That's heavy enough that putting it away after every use is a chore, so you'll want a spot where it can stay out. Measure using the 40 cm with-handle depth, and leave room to pull the basket out toward you.

Power. It's a high-output appliance with a power consumption of 1500W. Don't use power strips, extension cords, or adapters; plug it directly into a wall outlet. You need a wall outlet close to where you plan to put it. It also helps to think ahead about what else shares that outlet.

Care. The basket and crisper plate are dishwasher-safe. Appliances that are a hassle to wash tend to stop getting used, so this is a real plus. Check also that the basket fits in your dishwasher.

Warranty. It comes with a two-year warranty.
