Following the aroma of coffee into a Vietnamese morning

Hello, I’m Robu.

Different cities, different cups, well-loved cafés and the words you need to order: this is a practical guide to enjoying coffee while travelling in Vietnam.

Opening hours, menus and prices can change. Check each café’s official website before visiting. The Japanese version includes approximate katakana pronunciation guides; the Vietnamese spellings below can be shown to staff.

Well-loved cafés

Café Giảng, Hanoi

A café known for egg coffee. Its official website lists several locations, including 39 Nguyễn Hữu Huân in the Old Quarter. It is an option for anyone wanting to try a characteristically Hanoi-style combination of egg cream and strong coffee.

96B café & roastery, Ho Chi Minh City

This roastery focuses on Vietnamese-grown Liberica/Excelsa, Robusta and Arabica, among others. Its official location information lists 96B Phan Ngữ, Tân Định. It is a good candidate if you want to compare different beans.

43 Factory Coffee Roaster, Da Nang

A specialty coffee shop focused on origins and brewing. Consider it on a day when you want to explore clean flavours and the stories behind the beans in Da Nang. Check the official page for the address and opening hours.

Familiar coffee styles to remember

  • cà phê sữa đá: strong coffee, condensed milk and ice.
  • cà phê đen đá: iced black coffee without condensed milk.
  • cà phê trứng: a Hanoi speciality topped with egg-yolk cream.
  • cà phê muối: coffee with a salty cream that brings out the sweetness and bitterness.

Useful phrases for ordering

The Vietnamese phrase and its English meaning are shown together so you can read them without hovering or tapping.

Cho tôi một cà phê sữa đá.One iced coffee with condensed milk, please.
Cho tôi một cà phê đen đá.One iced black coffee, please.
Ít đường.Less sugar.
Không đường.No sugar.
Không sữa.No milk or condensed milk.
Uống ở đây.I’ll drink it here.
Mang đi.To take away.
Bao nhiêu tiền?How much is it?

A small tip when ordering

If you are concerned about sweetness, say Ít đường or Không đường at the start. If pronunciation makes communication difficult, showing the Vietnamese text on this page to a staff member can help you confirm your order.
