Why the Peacock WHA-40 Has My Attention: Hygienic Steam, a 4L Tank, and Up to 26 Hours

The Peacock WHA-40 steam humidifier has really caught my attention.

The main reason is simple: it heats water into steam, a method the manufacturer says helps suppress the growth of mold and bacteria and makes the unit easier to use hygienically. A humidifier releases moisture directly into the air around us, so the humidification method is the first thing I want to understand.

The WHA-40 also combines a 4.0-liter tank with up to about 26 hours of operation on low. It has a published quietness figure, a substantial set of safety features, and a dark-gray body with vertical shinogi-style ribs that looks at home in a Japanese-style room. Here is how those features translate into everyday life.

Hygienic steam made by heating water

The WHA-40 is a heated-steam humidifier. Peacock positions the method itself as a central benefit: heating the water helps suppress the growth of mold and bacteria, making the humidifier easier to use with hygiene in mind.

The outlet is designed with heat in mind as well. Air is mixed with steam heated to around 100°C, bringing the outlet temperature down to about 67°C. It is still a steam appliance and the outlet should not be touched, but the design considers both the hygiene of heated steam and everyday household use.

Output and room size matter, but because this is something I would run every day, the hygienic approach is what first draws me to the WHA-40.

A 4L tank and up to about 26 hours of continuous operation

The tank holds 4.0 liters. With a full tank at a room temperature of 20°C, the approximate running times are:

Setting Continuous running time
High About 8 hours
Medium About 14 hours
Low About 26 hours

Fill it before bed and start it on low, and it should still be running in the morning; on paper, it can continue into the following evening. Even high offers about eight hours, which fits neatly into a daytime block or a night's sleep.

Fewer refills can make a real difference with an appliance used every day. A refill lamp shows when the water is running low, while the removable lid and wide opening should make refilling straightforward.

Humidification output and room size

Output is about 480 mL/h on high, 290 mL/h on medium, and 155 mL/h on low. On high, the room-size guideline is up to 13 tatami mats for a prefabricated Western-style room and 8 tatami mats for a wooden Japanese-style room.

High can serve a living room or larger Western-style room, while medium and low can be selected to suit a bedroom, tatami room, or the day's level of dryness.

Under the specified conditions of a full tank, 20°C water, a 20°C room, and AC 100V, humidification begins after about 25 minutes. In everyday terms, that is roughly the time between arriving home, switching it on, changing clothes, and starting dinner.

Power use and quiet operation

Power consumption is 1,200W while boiling and about 400 to 145W during humidification. The unit uses its highest wattage to heat the water, then drops once humidification is underway.

The published operating noise on high is about 37 dB, excluding the boiling period. For a bedroom or study, having a clear noise figure makes it easier to judge where the unit might fit.

A substantial set of safety features

Steam appliances benefit from thoughtful safety design, and the WHA-40 includes:

  • A magnetic power cord
  • Spill reduction if the unit tips over
  • Automatic shutoff when tilted or tipped
  • A double-lock lid
  • A child lock
  • Boil-dry protection and a refill lamp

The magnetic cord reduces the chance of the whole unit being dragged if someone catches the cable, while both the lid and controls have locks. These features add reassurance when the humidifier is used on a flat, stable surface with hands and faces kept away from the steam outlet.

Dark gray styling that suits a Japanese-style room

The finish is dark gray, with vertical shinogi-style ribs that create a quiet play of light across the straight-sided body. It looks calm enough for both a wood-floored living room and a tatami room.

The unit measures about 24.5 × 26.8 × 35.9 cm, weighs about 3.3 kg, and has a power cord around 1.0 meter long. Thinking about the full-tank weight, steam direction, and outlet position in advance will make placement easier.

A wide opening and filterless care

There is no filter to replace, and the lid comes off to expose a wide opening. Emptying the remaining water and wiping away moisture should be done according to the instruction manual. The appeal is a simple structure with easy access to the inside.
