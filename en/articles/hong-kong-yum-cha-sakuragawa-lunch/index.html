Hong Kong Dim Sum Lunch in Sakuragawa, Osaka: Comparing Two Set Meals

Hello, I’m Robu.

On August 9, 2026, I visited Hong Kong Yum Cha Sakuragawa, located between JR Namba and Sakuragawa stations in Osaka.

The restaurant serves steamed dim sum as well as congee, Hong Kong-style fried noodles, rice dishes, desserts and full courses. We ordered one Hong Kong Yum Cha Set and one Sakuragawa Set, then added a mango dessert à la carte.

Note: This is an independent account of a meal I paid for and experienced myself. The restaurant did not commission or pay for this article. Only the Hot Pepper Gourmet reservation link near the end is an affiliate advertisement.

The short version

This is a convenient lunch option for anyone who wants to sample several kinds of Hong Kong-style food in one sitting. The Hong Kong Yum Cha Set includes fried noodles, while the Sakuragawa Set includes pork buns and tapioca mango milk.

Ordering one of each let us compare the two combinations. The food had an authentic feel and, most importantly, tasted good.

Location

The restaurant is at 1-1-29 Sakuragawa, Naniwa Ward, Osaka. Its official website lists it as about a four-minute walk from JR Namba’s north exit and about five minutes from Osaka Metro Namba Exit 26 or Sakuragawa Exit 7.

The official website introduces the proprietor as being from Hong Kong and says the dim sum is handmade. This article uses that verified description rather than making assumptions about anyone’s background.

The two lunch sets

Hong Kong Yum Cha Set — ¥1,400 including tax

  • Three assorted steamed dim sum
  • Dried-scallop white congee
  • Hong Kong-style fried noodles
  • Pan-fried turnip cake

Sakuragawa Set — ¥1,600 including tax

  • Three assorted steamed dim sum
  • Dried-scallop white congee
  • Pork buns
  • Pan-fried turnip cake
  • Tapioca mango milk

These were the prices shown when we visited. Menus and prices may change.

An à la carte mango dessert

After the set meals, we separately ordered the Mango Pudding with Tapioca and Mango Milk. The photographed menu listed it at ¥820 including tax.

This is separate from the simpler tapioca mango milk included in the Sakuragawa Set.

A broad menu beyond lunch

The menu covers individual dim sum, noodles, congee, rice dishes, side dishes, desserts and Shaoxing wine. At the time of our visit, it also showed three courses priced at ¥3,500, ¥4,800 and ¥6,200.

Rather than filling this article with menu photographs, I have included only the image that compares the two lunch sets. The remaining menu photographs are retained as research material.

What to know before visiting

  • The two sets offer different priorities: fried noodles in the Hong Kong Yum Cha Set, and pork buns plus a sweet course in the Sakuragawa Set.
  • You can sample dim sum, congee and turnip cake in a single meal.
  • The restaurant is within walking distance of JR Namba, Namba and Sakuragawa stations.
  • We visited for Sunday lunch on August 9, 2026, but the current official page lists weekend hours as dinner only. Opening hours may have changed, so check before visiting.
  • Prices and set contents may change.
  • The official page lists no dedicated parking.

Robu’s Take

Hong Kong Yum Cha Sakuragawa was an enjoyable place to sample several kinds of dim sum near Namba. Ordering one of each lunch set gave us both the fried noodles and the pork buns, and the additional mango pudding completed the meal with a memorable dessert.

The food felt authentic and was delicious. On a future visit, I would like to try more of the individual dim sum or one of the larger courses.
