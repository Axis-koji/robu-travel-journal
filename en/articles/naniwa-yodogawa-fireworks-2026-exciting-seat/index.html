Naniwa Yodogawa Fireworks 2026: The Up-Close Impact of the Exciting Seat Area

On Saturday, October 17, 2026, I watched the 38th Naniwa Yodogawa Fireworks Festival from the paid Exciting Seat area. In short, the fireworks appeared so close that my neck ached, while the show’s blend of music and fireworks kept me moved from beginning to end.

I had previously watched from free locations such as the riverbank, but this time I decided to splurge. This article separates the festival’s official details from my own experience.

The Exciting Seat area before opening

Festival and Exciting Seat Basics

Here are the details listed on the official website.

Item Details
Event 38th Naniwa Yodogawa Fireworks Festival
Date Saturday, October 17, 2026
Fireworks time 7:00 PM–8:00 PM
Venue Yodo River bank near Shinkitano 3-chome, Yodogawa Ward, Osaka
Exciting Seat price Adults ¥14,000 / Children ¥7,000
Gates open 4:00 PM
Capacity Up to 10,000 people
Included Bento meal, tea, table, and folding chair
Seating type Unreserved seating within a designated area

The official guide describes the Exciting Seat area as a central location close to the launch site, where viewers can feel the impact of large shells and starmines. The official FAQ also recommends the Exciting Seats and the cruise boats for the clearest view of the waterside fireworks.

This Time, I Chose the Paid Exciting Seat Area

Until now, I had watched fireworks from free locations such as the riverbank. For this festival, I chose the paid Exciting Seat area and decided to splurge.

Unreserved Seating: I Arrived About 40 Minutes Before Opening

The Exciting Seat area is designated, but seating within it is unreserved. The gates opened at 4:00 PM, and I arrived about 40 minutes earlier and joined the line.

A Bento and Tea Bearing the Festival’s Name

The seat package includes a bento and tea. The box I received was printed with the name “Naniwa Yodogawa Fireworks.”

The bento box with the festival’s name

The bento was delicious. There were food stalls at the venue, but the included meal was enough for me.

The bento provided with the Exciting Seat

Fireworks Right in Front of Me, in Sync with the Music

The fireworks began at 7:00 PM.

What stayed with me most was the closeness and immediacy—as if I were watching from where the fireworks were being launched. They appeared to fill the sky directly in front of me, close enough to leave my neck aching.

Fireworks seen over the crowd

The performance, with its music and fireworks working as one, kept me moved from start to finish.

I took photos and videos, but they could not fully capture the impact of the fireworks appearing right in front of me.

For Anyone Considering a Paid Seat for the First Time

Based on my experience and the official information, these are the main points to consider.

  • Price: At the 2026 festival, ¥14,000 for adults and ¥7,000 for children.
  • Seating: Unreserved seating within a designated area.
  • Arrival: Gates opened at 4:00 PM. I arrived and lined up about 40 minutes earlier.
  • Included: Bento, tea, a table, and a folding chair. The bento was delicious, and I did not need anything from the food stalls.
  • Closeness and impact: The fireworks appeared to loom in front of me, and the music and fireworks felt fully integrated.

These prices and conditions are based on the 2026 festival. Please check the official website for current sales procedures and conditions for future events.

Robu's Wrap-Up

After previously watching from free locations such as the riverbank, this was the first time I chose to splurge on the Exciting Seat area. The closeness and immediacy made it feel as though I were watching from the launch site itself. The fireworks appeared so close that my neck ached, and the music-synchronized performance kept me moved throughout.

My photos and videos could not fully preserve that impact. The area had designated but unreserved seating, and I joined the line about 40 minutes before the 4:00 PM opening. Anyone considering a paid seat can use that arrival time as one reference point alongside the price and included amenities.
