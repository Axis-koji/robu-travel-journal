Why I enjoy the Soundcore Space One Pro after losing earbuds

Sound quality is not the only reason to choose a pair of headphones.

For me, another reason was wanting to worry less about losing one earbud. I had used true wireless earbuds, only to find that one of the pair was missing.

On the train, I would worry about accidentally touching my ear and knocking an earbud out. What if it fell from the platform onto the tracks? Those thoughts bothered me.

The headphones I now enjoy using are Anker’s Soundcore Space One Pro.

This is not a comparison with other headphones. These are my own impressions from using them on trains, in my workplace break room and occasionally at home.

Quiet is welcome when I want to think

Noise cancellation is my favourite feature.

When I wear these in the break room, colleagues sometimes ask, “What music are you listening to?”

My answer is, “I’m not listening to anything. It’s silence.” I am not playing music; I am using noise cancellation to think quietly.

At first they look surprised. Then they say something like, “But you do need that, don’t you?” It is an unusual little exchange, but it captures what I want from these headphones.

When background noise bothers me, they feel so quiet that I want to call it “silence.” I do not mean that every sound disappears completely. I simply appreciate that sense of quiet when I want to think.

For me, they are not only a way to enjoy music. They make it easier to have some time to myself.

They get warm in summer, but the setting matters

Because they cover my ears, they do get warm. Outdoors in hot weather I sweat, and that is not particularly comfortable.

However, I mostly use them on air-conditioned trains or in the air-conditioned break room at work. I also use them at home when noise bothers me.

Before buying them, I thought summer use might be difficult. In my actual routine, the warmth has bothered me less than I expected.

Walking somewhere hot and sitting in a cool room are quite different experiences.

Less worry about dropping one earbud

Earbuds are small, easy to carry and do not cover the whole ear. Even so, I worried about losing one.

Since switching to headphones, I no longer have that particular worry about one earbud falling out. Of course, I still need to remember not to leave the headphones behind.

It is not something that appears in a specification table, but that peace of mind matters in everyday use.

A short charge is convenient

I do not always wait for a full charge.

When the battery is low, I charge them for around five to ten minutes and use them again. I find that helpful.

I have not measured playback time after each charging interval. What I appreciate is not having to wait very long when I want to use them for a while.

I also like that they fold

I like being able to fold them when they are not in use.

I filmed myself opening them. Handling them while holding a phone in one hand is quite difficult; folding them one-handed was particularly awkward for me.

The video shows both their compact folded shape and what handling them is actually like.

I have not tried the wired connection yet

I normally use Bluetooth.

My smartphone has a headphone jack, so I like having the option of a wired connection. However, I have not tried using the cable. This article does not evaluate wired sound quality or usability.

A good fit for my daily life

I cannot say whether these are the best headphones for everyone. I have not compared them with other brands.

Even so, I really like them.

The quiet when I want to think. Less worry about losing one earbud. Being able to use them again after a short charge. And being able to fold them.

They feel hot and stuffy outdoors in warm weather, but they suit me because I mostly use them on air-conditioned trains and in the break room.

Beyond the numbers on a specification sheet, what matters to me is whether something feels comfortable to use in my own life. That is why I like these headphones.
