Tiger HYBRID BREW ADG-A060: Four Brews, Starting With Freshly Ground Aroma

You put beans into the grinder and press the button. The sound of grinding rises with the aroma, and then brewing begins. I think the joy of a fully automatic coffee maker lies not only in the taste but also in this whole sequence, in watching it unfold while you wait.

The HYBRID BREW ADG-A060, released on October 1, 2026, is a fully automatic coffee maker from Tiger Corporation of Kadoma, Osaka. Its signature feature is “hybrid brewing,” which combines two brewing methods, percolation and immersion, in a single machine.

I should say up front that I haven’t tried the actual unit yet. This article is based on Tiger’s official product page and press release, and it’s about the kind of coffee time this machine looks set to offer.

1. Hybrid Brewing: Percolation and Immersion in One Machine

Percolation is the drip method, where hot water passes through a bed of grounds. Immersion steeps the grounds fully in hot water to draw out their components, much like brewing with a French press.

According to Tiger, percolation draws out bitterness, acidity, and aroma, while immersion draws out sweetness and body. Hybrid brewing combines the two, with the idea of layering the strengths of each into a single cup.

As a coffee lover, I find it exciting that a company with a long history of mastering heat in thermos bottles is taking on brewing in this way. Drip and press are usually enjoyed with separate tools, and here both methods fit into one fully automatic machine.

2. A Stepless Conical Burr Grinder, From Fine to Coarse

The grinder is a stepless conical burr type. Tiger describes it in terms of grind-size adjustment, low off-flavors, and aroma.

What I find most fun is that you can freely adjust the grind to suit your taste and your beans. Because it isn’t limited to fixed steps, you can range from fine to coarse. You might try a slightly coarser grind for a dark roast, or a finer one for a light roast. Even with the same beans, a small change in grind can change the impression in the cup, and exploring that difference is part of the pleasure of grinding your own beans.

When you bring home a new bag of beans, you could start with a medium grind, then change it slightly the next day. That seems like a lovely way to get to know a single bag from top to bottom.

3. Four Brew Menus to Match Your Mood

There are four menus: Rich, Strong, Decaf, and Iced. Each has a flavor profile described on the official product page, and each uses a different brewing method.

Rich is the only one of the four that uses hybrid brewing. Officially, it is described as a mellow cup that brings out the natural flavor of the beans in good balance. It looks like the standard cup, the one that best shows off what this machine is about.

Strong brews by percolation. It is described as a bold flavor suited to waking up in the morning. It sounds right for those mornings when you want a cup that straightens your back and starts the day crisply.

Decaf is an immersion menu, and it is described as bringing rich flavor even from decaffeinated beans. It’s a dependable choice after dinner or on a quiet evening with a book, when you want to enjoy the aroma of the beans.

Iced is also an immersion menu. Officially, its appeal is body, a pleasant bitterness, and a clean aftertaste. It seems made for a hot afternoon, but also for a break between tasks with a glass of ice in hand.

Strong in the morning, Iced in the afternoon, Decaf at night. Being able to choose by mood and time of day is the kind of enjoyment only a machine with four faces can offer.

4. A Capacity That Fits Just Right, From 1 to 6 Cups

It brews 1 to 6 cups, with one cup being about 120 mL. The rated capacity is 0.85 L.

Whether you want a single cup alone in the morning, a relaxed coffee with your family after a meal, or coffee for friends who have dropped by, one machine lets you choose the amount. Because it’s fully automatic, the flow of grinding and brewing stays the same as the number of people grows.

Carefully hand-dripping six cups for guests takes effort and attention. Being able to serve them all at once, with the aroma of freshly ground beans, is a real convenience.

5. A Slim 16 cm Vertical Design, and Power Use

The dimensions are about 16 cm wide × 33.3 cm deep × 41.2 cm high. Its slim vertical shape makes it easy to place even at the edge of a kitchen counter. It weighs about 3.6 kg, and the power cord is about 1 m long.

The rated power consumption is 911 W. If you use it at the same time as appliances that draw a lot of power, such as a microwave or a toaster, it is reassuring to pay a little attention to your breaker capacity.

6. Detachable Parts for Easy Care, and Dishwasher Compatibility

Ease of care matters for anything you use every day. The water tank is detachable, so you can carry it straight to the sink and fill it. The grinder mechanism comes off as a whole unit, and the outer and inner burrs can be taken apart. Being able to clean the places where grounds tend to collect is a satisfying detail.

On top of that, many of the parts can go in the dishwasher. The compatible parts are the hopper, the hopper lid, the filter, the server lid, and the coffee server.

After you have finished your coffee, you can put the server and filter into the dishwasher along with the rest, and they will be washed while you clear up after dinner. The grinder mechanism is not dishwasher-compatible, so that part is detached and cleaned separately. Even so, being able to leave most of the parts you wash to the dishwasher is a welcome feature for keeping up the habit every day.

7. Price, Specs, and Who I’d Recommend It To

The price shown at Tiger’s official online store is 39,600 yen (tax included), as confirmed on October 29, 2026. The press release lists it as an open price, and prices and stock may change. It comes in Onyx Black.

Tiger’s official news also reports that it won a 2026 Good Design Award.

Who I’d recommend HYBRID BREW to

  • People who want to grind beans fresh and enjoy the aroma along with the coffee
  • People who want to explore the differences in grind and brewing method
  • People who want to choose a menu to suit their mood, morning, noon, or night
  • People who want to fit a machine into a limited kitchen space in a slim form
  • People who want to leave cleanup to the dishwasher and brew casually every day

Points to check before you buy

  • The width, depth, and height of the spot where you will put it (the height is about 41.2 cm)
  • Which parts are dishwasher-safe, and the fact that the grinder mechanism is cleaned by hand
  • Your breaker capacity if you will run it together with a microwave or toaster

According to the official product specifications, the country of production is China. It is not made in Japan, but it is a machine built by a Japanese manufacturer from Osaka, with thought given to its brewing method and ease of use.

Robu's Take

For anyone who wants to enjoy the whole flow of making coffee, from the aroma of grinding to watching the brew to savoring the cup, I think the HYBRID BREW is a very fitting machine.

You can choose among Rich, Strong, Decaf, and Iced according to your mood and the time of day. With the stepless grinder, you can change the grind and deepen your relationship with your beans. The slim 16 cm shape is easy to picture in a kitchen, and the detachable parts and dishwasher-compatible parts look like they will make daily care easy to keep up.

A strong cup to wake up in the morning, a glass of iced coffee for a break in the afternoon, and a quiet decaf in the evening to enjoy the aroma. I get the feeling this one machine would gently support a whole day of coffee time. Buy a bag of beans, change the grind a little each time, and find your favorite cup. I would love to enjoy that someday myself.
