Panasonic ER-SC6A: 7 Checks Before Buying

I am currently considering Panasonic's Lamdash clipper ER-SC6A as a purchase candidate. The exact model number is ER-SC6A-S, in Titanium Silver, and it goes on sale in early November 2026 (retailers list November 1).

For anyone thinking about cutting their own hair or trimming a family member's, a clipper with Japan-made blades, a washable body, and fine length control already looks appealing. But a list of specs doesn't show how it fits into daily life. So this guide uses official information and breaks it into seven items to check before buying.

The ER-SC6A comes in color variants marked by a suffix on the model number. This article covers ER-SC6A-S (Titanium Silver). It goes on sale in early November 2026, and retailers show November 1.

On the price, when I checked on November 21, 2026, the Panasonic official Yahoo! store listed model ER-SC6A-S at 16,984 yen including tax, with free shipping. Rakuten also has a matching listing from the official Panasonic Store Plus shop.

2. Motor and Blades: Pro-Grade Linear Motor, Japan-Made Blades

At its core is the same high-performance linear motor used in Panasonic's professional clippers. It drives the movable blade directly at about 9,800 strokes per minute and keeps its power until just before the battery runs out, supporting a smooth cut from start to finish. The benefit is not simply a powerful motor; it is the ability to keep blade speed steady throughout the session.

The Japan-made blade system gives each part a clear role. The fixed blade gathers the hair, while the carbon-coated movable blade, sharpened to a 45-degree edge angle, cuts it. That combination of drive power and carefully made blades explains what Panasonic means by pro-grade performance.

3. Length Settings: 39 Levels in 0.5 mm Dial Steps

Two comb attachments are included: one for 1-10 mm and one for 11-20 mm. Fit the attachment for the range you want, then turn the dial in the center of the body to choose from 39 settings in 0.5 mm steps. The A and B markings on the attachments correspond to the dial scale, so you can line up the exact number you want.

Without an attachment, the straight blade cuts to about 0.5 mm. The available range runs from 1 to 20 mm, with one unusual gap: 10.5 mm cannot be selected because it falls between the two attachment ranges.

The supported styles are buzz cut, very short, short, and two-block. I usually ask my barber for a two-block style, so I am especially interested in using it to tidy the sides and neckline at home. Dial control also makes small changes—such as one millimeter shorter than usual—easy to picture.

4. Waterproofing and Cleaning: IPX7, Rinse the Whole Body

The waterproof rating is IPX7, and the whole body can be rinsed under water. It also supports water-through cleaning, which lets you rinse the blades without removing them.

With clippers, cleanup often takes longer than cutting. When fine hair clings to the blades and body, cleaning eats into your time. If you can rinse it off, you can wash the hair away in the bathroom or at the sink and finish quickly, which shortens the cleanup path. Whether a tool is easy to keep using is a key factor, so I count this as a real plus.

5. Charging and Runtime: About 2 Hours to Charge, About 120 Minutes of Use

The body is rechargeable. Charging takes about two hours, and runtime is about 120 minutes. Those figures apply to dry use at 20 to 30 degrees Celsius.

For a home haircut, a single session is not long, so 120 minutes leaves plenty of margin. If you make a habit of charging after each use, you reduce the risk of finding the battery empty when you need it.

The charging cable is included, but the USB power adapter is sold separately. Panasonic lists the compatible ES-RU1-01 at a suggested retail price of 3,850 yen including tax. Decide whether to use an adapter you already own or order the compatible unit so you can start using the clipper as soon as it arrives.

6. Size, Weight, and Accessories: About 185 g, Semi-Hard Case Included

The body measures about 180 x 48 x 49 mm and weighs about 185 g. A semi-hard case is included.

At about 185 g, it weighs roughly as much as a typical smartphone. That familiar comparison makes it easier to imagine holding it while reaching the back and sides of your head. The included semi-hard case keeps the body and attachments together and should make bathroom-shelf or drawer storage straightforward.

7. Preparing to Enjoy Self-Cutting

To make the most of the ER-SC6A-S's fine length control, it helps to prepare your space before cutting. Mirrors, a cape, and a clear cleanup route let you take your time and enjoy the process.

  • Use two mirrors. In addition to the front mirror, a hand mirror or a second mirror helps you check the back of your head and neckline.
  • Wear a cape. It keeps hair out of your clothes. A trash bag with holes cut for the head and arms works as a substitute.
  • Plan your cleanup route. Cutting in the bathroom or laying newspaper on the floor makes cleanup easier to keep up.
  • Ask a family member to help. The back of your head is hard to see yourself, so letting a trusted family member handle it is a real option.
  • Start with a longer setting. Cutting shorter is easy, but you can't undo it. Even out the whole head at a longer length first, then go shorter as needed.

The 0.5 mm dial steps suit this approach of shortening a little at a time. Moving toward your preferred length in stages is part of what makes the 39 settings useful.

Robu's Take

The appeal of the ER-SC6A-S lies in its pro-grade linear motor, Japan-made blades, fine 0.5 mm length steps, and IPX7 body that you can rinse whole. The reasons to buy come down to three:

  • You value the fundamentals of a clipper: cutting performance and ease of use.
  • You want to use a dial to fine-tune your preferred length.
  • You want to cut down on cleanup so that haircuts become a habit.

If needed, add the separately sold ES-RU1-01 USB power adapter to your purchase plan. The unusual missing 10.5 mm setting is also useful to know when picturing the lengths you use most. Before ordering, compare the model number ER-SC6A-S and the JAN code 4550719226995 once more.

If it makes cutting hair at home even a little more relaxed, this clipper is a strong candidate.
