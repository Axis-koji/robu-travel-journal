Less Trash-Emptying After Every Clean: Why Shark's Cordless Vacuum Stands Out

After you finish vacuuming, there is that extra step of emptying the dust cup. Wouldn't getting the vacuum out feel a little lighter if that step mostly went away?

Emptying the bin, closer to a monthly task

The star of this vacuum is its auto-empty dock. When you finish cleaning, you simply return the vacuum to the dock, and the collected debris is moved into it. In the manufacturer's testing, the dock can hold about 30 days' worth of dirt, so the trash-emptying that used to follow every session is reduced.

The amount of dirt differs from home to home, so the number is a guideline. Even so, spending less time holding a dust cup over the bin can make a real difference in daily cleaning. With less hassle afterward, it becomes easier to pick up the vacuum the moment you notice a mess. That is what I liked most about it.

Hair and different floors, everyday small worries

The design also takes on the annoyance of hair wrapping around the brush. The head supports multiple floor types, and the manufacturer describes a hair-resistant design based on its testing. Being able to move from hardwood to a rug with the same head is reassuring when you clean as you move through the house.

In a home with long hair, removing strands wrapped around the brush is another small chore. This design looks promising as a way to reduce that maintenance.

Up to about 13 minutes, for quick everyday cleaning

Continuous run time is up to about 13 minutes as a stick and up to about 17 minutes as a handheld. Charging takes about 3.5 hours.

Those numbers look well suited to quickly cleaning a spot the moment you notice it: the dining area in the morning, the entryway after coming home, or the kitchen after a meal. If you clean in short, frequent rounds, you use only what you need and then return the vacuum to its dock.

It is easy to picture this model fitting people who add a little cleaning throughout the day rather than doing the whole house at once.

Size and weight

As a stick it measures 1030 × 267 × 124 mm and weighs about 1.9 kg. As a handheld it is 500 × 67 × 86.5 mm and about 0.9 kg. Stored, it takes up 1057 × 410 × 257 mm, so it helps to decide where the dock will go. The handheld looks useful for shelves, car interiors, and stair corners.
