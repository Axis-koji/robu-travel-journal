Moisture Care for Hair, Scalp, and Skin: 7 Reasons the Panasonic EH-NB70 Stands Out

Panasonic launched the Nanocare EH-NB70 hair dryer in Japan on November 1, 2026. Its compact body brings together fast drying, nanoe MOISTURE+, double smart sensing, and four care modes—turning an everyday drying routine into time for beauty care.

I have not purchased or used this product. This article introduces its features using Panasonic's official product pages, specifications, and feature guides.

1. About 565 g and 35% Less Volume in a Compact Everyday Design

The EH-NB70 measures 21.7 × 9.6 × 7.7 cm and weighs about 565 g. Compared with the earlier EH-NA0K, its body volume is about 35% smaller, making it Panasonic's most compact Nanocare model to date.

The short head creates a clean, easy-to-handle form for aiming airflow toward the back of the head and the ends. It should also fit neatly into a washroom storage space or travel bag.

2. About 1.2x the Drying Performance of the EH-NA0K

A small high-speed motor gives the EH-NB70 about 1.2x the drying performance of the EH-NA0K. Panasonic has improved drying speed while making the body smaller—a combination that can make busy mornings and evening hair care feel lighter.

3. 1.9x the Moisture for Hair with nanoe MOISTURE+

The EH-NB70 combines nanoe MOISTURE+, mineral ions, and negative ions. Moisture-rich nanoe particles enter the tiny gaps between cuticles to moisturize both the surface and the inside of the hair.

In Panasonic's hair-tress evaluation, a nanoe MOISTURE+-equipped model delivered 1.9x the moisture to hair compared with a conventional nanoe-equipped model. The result is designed to support shine, manageability, and a smoother feel while drying.

The same combination helps improve cuticle adhesion, which suppresses color loss from dyed or gray-covered hair. It also helps reduce damage from UV exposure and friction, adding everyday care to the basic drying routine.

4. Double Smart Sensing Helps Keep the Air from Becoming Too Hot

Double smart sensing detects both room temperature and air temperature, then automatically controls the heat. It is designed to reduce excessive heat while drying across changing seasons and room conditions.

The specified air temperature is about 80°C in strong/HOT mode at a room temperature of 30°C. More important than the number alone is the automatic temperature control working in the background every day.

5. Four Modes for Hair, Ends, Scalp, and Skin

The EH-NB70 offers four modes for different finishing and care routines.

  • Hot/Cold Rhythm: Alternates warm and cool air automatically to support a smooth, glossy finish.
  • Intensive Care: Focuses on damaged ends as a finishing step after the hair is about 90% dry.
  • Scalp: Uses gentle air at about 60°C to dry the scalp comfortably while nanoe MOISTURE+, minerals, and negative ions add moisture.
  • Skin: After drying the hair, direct the air toward the face for about one minute to help keep post-bath skin moisturized.

Use fast drying in the morning, then take more time for the ends, scalp, and skin at night. That flexibility is one of the EH-NB70's most appealing everyday qualities.

6. 1200 W, AC 100 V, and a 1.7 m Cord

The dryer is rated at 1200 W on AC 100 V, 50–60 Hz, with a cord about 1.7 m long. A set nozzle is included.

Panasonic instructs users to connect it directly to a dedicated outlet rated at 15 A or higher. Checking the outlet and cord reach in the intended washroom space helps make setup straightforward.

7. Four Colors Designed to Add Soft Color to Everyday Life

The regular range comes in Sheer Beige, Amber Black, and Misty Rose. Warm White is a Panasonic Store Plus exclusive. The understated palette is designed to sit naturally in a modern beauty space.

Part of the body uses a biomass paint containing plant-derived ingredients. Panasonic Store Plus lists the dryer at ¥40,590 including tax, and the product is manufactured in Thailand. It is positioned as a high-performance Nanocare model that brings together hair care, fast drying, and compactness.
