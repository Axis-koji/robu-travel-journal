Aladdin Dry Circulator: Fan, Warm Air and Laundry Drying in One Year-Round Appliance

Aladdin Dry Circulator AFH-HC12A. Image: official Japan AIC press material via PR TIMES.

Aladdin's first circulator combines summer airflow, winter foot-level warmth and rainy-day laundry drying in one appliance. That three-in-one idea is what caught my attention. Rather than storing it away after each season, I can imagine leaving it in a living room or dressing area and finding a use for it throughout the year.

I have not handled the product in person. This article uses Aladdin's official information to picture how it might fit into everyday life.

Dry laundry from directly underneath

The Dry Circulator is designed to sit below hanging laundry and send air upward. Aladdin reports that its strong warm-air drying mode shortened drying time by about 83% compared with natural drying in a manufacturer test. The conditions were a room equivalent to 5.5 tatami mats, about 20°C, about 70% humidity and a 2 kg load, so results will vary at home.

Its four laundry modes combine weak or strong warm air with weak or strong fan-only airflow. That makes it easy to picture fan-only drying in the rainy season and warm-air drying in colder months.

Automatic shutoff if laundry falls

Two infrared sensors form Aladdin's W shut-off sensor system. It is designed to detect a fallen item and turn the appliance off automatically. It also includes a double-safety tip-over switch, overheating protection and a 104°C thermal fuse.

Aladdin is a Japanese brand operated by Japan AIC, headquartered in Kasai, Hyogo. Its long experience with heating appliances gives the safety design particular credibility.

A year-round air-circulation scene. Image: official Japan AIC press material via PR TIMES.

1,200W warm air and a dedicated foot mode

Warm-air consumption is 1,200W, with low, medium and high output. Foot mode angles the air 12 degrees downward. In Aladdin's test, strong warm air produced a 5°C temperature increase at a height of 30 cm up to about 2 metres away—roughly 1.6 times the reach of straight-ahead airflow under the same condition.

I can picture it under a desk or in a kitchen, warming the place where someone is standing rather than trying to heat an entire room.

Designed for dressing rooms and bedrooms

The IP24 rating supports use around kitchens and dressing areas as described by Aladdin. A quiet DC fan measures about 34 dB at the weakest setting, making bedtime use easier to imagine.

  • A magnetic remote that attaches to the unit
  • A removable, washable filter
  • 147-degree vertical and 150-degree horizontal swing
  • 0.5, 2, 4 and 8-hour off timers

These details help turn three headline functions into something that looks easy to live with.

Use it with air conditioning in summer

In warm weather it becomes a five-speed fan and air circulator, including use with air conditioning. Model AFH-HC12A comes in green and white, measures 302 × 243 × 328 mm and weighs about 3.2 kg.

The launch estimate was around ¥30,580 including tax. That is not inexpensive for a circulator alone, but this product also provides warm air and laundry drying while reducing the need to store and swap seasonal appliances.

Robu's Take

What appeals to me is not merely that the Dry Circulator has three jobs. Each one reflects Aladdin's heating expertise: airflow from directly below laundry, automatic shutoff if something falls, warm air aimed at the feet, and a build suited to dressing areas and bedrooms.

I especially picture it in a cold dressing room at night and under laundry on a rainy day. On wet days it can sit beneath the laundry, in winter it can warm my feet, and in spring or summer it can circulate conditioned air. If it stays useful across the seasons, it earns the space it occupies.
