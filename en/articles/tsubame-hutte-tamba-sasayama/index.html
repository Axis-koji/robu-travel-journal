A Quiet Stop at Tsubame Hutte in Tamba-Sasayama | Green Views, Woodcraft, and Mint

Hello, I'm Robu.

In July, I stopped by Tsubame Hutte in Tamba-Sasayama, Hyogo Prefecture. It brings a café and a gallery of furniture and everyday objects together under one roof. I arrived about half an hour before closing, but even that short visit left me with the view through the windows, a pleasant conversation with the staff, and a memorable welcome from Mint, the café's Labrador retriever.

Mint greeted me at the door

Mint was the first character to make an impression. He greeted me when I opened the door and saw me off when I left. The official website introduces him as the café's dog; after meeting him, it is easy to see why he comes up so quickly when I recall this visit.

Hot chai and affogato by the window

Beyond the large gridded windows was the green landscape of the satoyama countryside. From the wooden table, the objects inside and the scenery outside seemed to belong together. I had hot chai and an affogato, and spent a little while simply looking out and talking with the staff.

Because I arrived so close to closing time, I would like to return with more time to spare. The staff suggested visiting in winter. The same window must frame a very different view then.

A café surrounded by woodwork

Wooden pieces are displayed and sold inside. Tsubame Hutte describes itself as a gallery and café featuring furniture by INABA WOOD WORKS and selected items. It also introduces woodwork workshops; check the official website for current details before planning one.

There were goats outside, too. By the time Mint saw me off, the animals, the woodwork, and the greenery had become parts of the same memory.

Robu's Take

Tsubame Hutte is more than a quick stop for a drink. The view, the wooden objects, and the conversation made even a brief visit feel unhurried. Next time, I would like to see the place in another season—perhaps winter.

Before you visit

Address: 843 Fukui, Tamba-Sasayama, Hyogo Prefecture, Japan.

Opening days, closing days, and the menu can change. Check the official Tsubame Hutte website shortly before you go.
