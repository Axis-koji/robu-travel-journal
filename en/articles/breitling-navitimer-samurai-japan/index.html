That indigo looks fantastic: the Navitimer GMT 41 Samurai Japan

This time, a Breitling watch caught my attention.

The first thing that catches my eye is the indigo.

Looking at the pictures, I imagine its appearance could change with the light. Then there is that single crimson GMT hand. I like that the Japanese character is expressed without being overstated. It looks rather good.

Limited to 100 pieces in Japan. The leather-strap version is ¥924,000 and the stainless-steel-bracelet version is ¥968,000.

A subtle expression of Japan in indigo and crimson

This is a Samurai Japan collaboration, but viewed from the front it does not look like an ostentatious commemorative piece. A single red hand against the indigo dial gives it a distinct character. It looks sporty without looking childish. They did not make every detail loud, and that balance appeals to me.

A GMT function for travelling abroad

The crimson GMT hand is not just decoration. It lets you check two time zones, such as Japan and your destination abroad. The dial remains relatively uncluttered while retaining the Navitimer’s familiar rotating slide rule.

A 41 mm case

The case measures 41 mm in diameter and 11.65 mm thick. Its automatic Calibre 32 has approximately 42 hours of power reserve. Water resistance is 3 bar, so I would not treat it as a sports watch designed for substantial water exposure.

Leather strap or bracelet

The leather strap offers a more understated feel. The bracelet is also appealing for summer and everyday use. The price difference is ¥44,000.

My thoughts

At over ¥900,000, it is expensive. Even so, the Navitimer design, GMT function and Japan-only run of 100 pieces could make it very appealing to someone who loves this kind of watch.

Personally, I would like to see how that indigo changes in different light on the actual watch.
