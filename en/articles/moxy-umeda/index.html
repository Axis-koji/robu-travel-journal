My stay at Moxy Osaka Umeda: breakfast choices and Moxy’s playful rooms

Hello, I’m Robu.

This time, I’m writing about my stay at Moxy Osaka Shin Umeda. Having stayed at Moxy hotels several times, I have become quite familiar with their distinctive, playful atmosphere.

I did not take many room photos on this visit, but I did photograph breakfast. There was plenty of variety, it tasted good, and I wanted to try a little of everything. Here are my impressions of breakfast and the convenient location.

About the photographs

The breakfast photographs were taken at Osaka Umeda. The room section uses reference photographs from an earlier stay at Moxy Kyoto Nijo. They do not show the actual rooms at Osaka Umeda and do not imply identical interiors or facilities.

A convenient base for exploring Fukushima and Umeda

The station was not far away, and nearby convenience stores made the location useful. According to the hotel’s official website, it is about a five-minute walk from JR Fukushima Station. It also makes a convenient base for exploring Umeda.

I enjoyed the hotel restaurant, but another attraction was the variety of places to eat nearby. It is nice to choose between eating at the hotel and going out, depending on your mood.

I had the impression that many guests were visiting from overseas. When I stayed, the price felt relatively reasonable compared with other Osaka city hotels. Rates vary by dates and plan, though, so compare the prices available for your own booking.

A breakfast with plenty to choose from

There was more choice than I expected: rice, miso soup, hot dishes, salad, bread and cereal. There were even doughnuts and juice—more variety than I could manage to eat.

The miso soup machine was a fun little detail. I liked having rice and miso soup as an option, rather than simply a light breakfast of bread and coffee.

Bread, cereal and something sweet

There were plenty of doughnuts too. I wanted something sweet after my meal, so I had some. Looking back at the photographs, I certainly made the most of breakfast.

The food tasted good, and the mix of Japanese and Western options made me think overseas guests could enjoy it as well.

The food and service described reflect my visit. Check your accommodation plan and the hotel’s current information for today’s menu, prices and whether breakfast is included.

Moxy’s playful room design

The Moxy hotels I have stayed at share a certain atmosphere. A little playfulness, rather than formality, is what stays with me.

Hanging chairs and folding luggage racks on the wall is another very Moxy-like touch. Taking them down when needed is an interesting idea, and I found it rather charming.

The room I stayed in on this visit had a shower rather than a bathtub. There were both overhead and handheld showers, and I felt refreshed afterwards. If you want to soak in a bath, check the room facilities before booking.

These reference photographs are from a different hotel and stay. Check the official room information for the size, layout and equipment of the room you intend to book at Osaka Umeda.

The benefits I received as a Platinum member on this stay

I am a Marriott Bonvoy Platinum member and often stay at Marriott hotels. On this visit to Moxy Osaka Umeda, I was able to use the following benefits.

  • A welcome-drink ticket
  • Vouchers for food and drink in the hotel. I stayed with my girlfriend, and we received vouchers for both of us.
  • Access to a coffee machine 24 hours a day

For a reference amount, the Marriott Bonvoy general benefit information lists a US$10 food-and-beverage credit per night as an Elite Welcome Gift option at Moxy hotels in Asia. That is the general information currently cited in the Japanese article, not confirmation of the value of the vouchers I received. Ask at check-in about the hotel’s specific offering and how many guests it covers.

For me, these benefits were part of the enjoyment of the stay. It felt like enjoying food-and-drink benefits and coffee instead of using a members-only lounge. That is my personal impression, not a statement that the hotel officially positions them as a lounge replacement. The hotel also has a café and bar, along with lounge space that is open to the public.

Some people choose hotels with member benefits in mind; others focus on location, price or the feel of the room. I think it helps to consider how the stay fits your own plans, rather than deciding only by the benefits available.

These are the benefits I used as a Platinum member on this particular stay. They are not services provided to every guest. Confirm membership eligibility, booking conditions, voucher restrictions and expiry dates with the hotel when booking or checking in. Reasons to return: familiar surroundings and breakfast

After several stays, the room’s atmosphere feels reassuringly familiar. Breakfast, meanwhile, offers the fun of deciding what to eat today.

Convenient places to eat nearby and a tasty hotel breakfast: that was my stay at Moxy Osaka Umeda.
