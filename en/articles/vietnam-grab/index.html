What to know before using Grab in Vietnam

Hello, I’m Robu.

Before getting in, review the process from booking to payment and the key precautions below.

App features, fares, service areas and payment methods may change. Prioritise the booking screen and Grab’s official information. Before departure, also check the Japanese Ministry of Foreign Affairs’ overseas safety information.

Before and during your ride

  1. Have a working SIM, eSIM or Wi-Fi connection.
  2. Place the pickup pin somewhere the vehicle can stop safely.
  3. Check the destination, service type such as GrabBike or GrabCar, and the displayed fare.
  4. After booking, match the number plate, vehicle model, colour and driver’s photograph to the app.
  5. Continue checking the route in the app during the journey, then pay using your selected method on arrival.

Important precautions

  • Do not get into a vehicle that does not match the app. If the vehicle, number plate or driver does not match, stay out and check through the app.
  • Trust the booking details, not unsolicited offers. Even if someone approaches you at an airport or tourist attraction, confirm the vehicle assigned in the app.
  • Be specific about the pickup point. Use shared landmarks such as the terminal, exit or pillar number.
  • Carry small notes if paying cash. Confirm how much cash you have handed over and check your change before leaving.
  • Do not immediately agree to unexpected extra charges. Check the app’s breakdown, tolls and booking details.
  • Wear your helmet properly on GrabBike. Consider a car if you have large bags or it is raining.
  • Avoid staring at your phone beside the road. Keep your belongings in front of you and wait somewhere safe.

Finding the pickup point at airports and large venues

There may be designated ride-hailing pickup areas. Compare the app’s instructions with signs on site, and keep communications with the driver inside the app. Check from a safe waiting area instead of walking into the road to look for the car.

Keep records if a problem occurs

Save the booking number, vehicle details, fare screen and in-app messages. Contact Grab support if there is a problem. Sharing your ride information with a travelling companion or family member can also be reassuring in case of an emergency.
