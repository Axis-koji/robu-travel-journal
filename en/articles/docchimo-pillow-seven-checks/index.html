Is the Docchimo Pillow Worth It? Seven Checks on Its ¥5,500 Two-Sided Design

If you like the idea of changing how your pillow feels from night to night, or enjoy soft pillows but dislike sinking too deeply, Docchimo Pillow may be worth considering. What catches my attention is the ability to choose between two materials simply by turning one pillow over.

This is not a hands-on sleep review. I reviewed Makura Co., Ltd.’s November 5, 2026 press release and official sales information to identify seven things to check before buying.

The official sales price is ¥5,500 including tax. Alongside the product’s features, I’ll also cover its official purchase page and the return and exchange questions worth checking.

1. Basic Specifications: Announced at ¥5,500, Made in Japan, Cover Not Included

The

manufacturer’s press release

names the product “どっちもピロー,” referred to here as Docchimo Pillow. Its release date is November 5, 2026, and its announced price is ¥5,500 including tax.

  • Dimensions: Approximately 63 cm wide × 43 cm deep, with a height of approximately 13–14 cm

  • Color: White

  • Filling: A microfiber and polyester batting blend on one side; soft pipe filling on the other

  • Outer fabric: 80% polyester, 20% cotton

  • Manufacturing: Made in Japan

  • Pillowcase: Not included

The product was planned by Makura Co., Ltd., based in Kashiwa, Chiba Prefecture. The manufacturer says sewing and finishing take place at factories in Japan.

When considering the price, I would remember that it covers the pillow itself. Checking whether an existing cover will fit, or whether I need a new one, helps make the purchase more concrete.

2. Two Sides and Two Layers: Softer or Firmer

The main feature is a reversible design with different materials and textures on each side.

The manufacturer calls the batting side “funwakame,” suggesting a soft, fluffy feel, and the pipe side “shikkarime,” suggesting a firmer feel. It describes the batting side as soft and enveloping, and the pipe side as slightly bumpy with a supportive feel around the neck.

According to the manufacturer, the pipe layer also acts as a foundation when the batting side faces upward, limiting how deeply the head sinks. Three horizontal quilting lines on the pipe side are designed to prevent the filling from shifting.

What interests me is being able to choose a softer or firmer surface without changing pillows. The pipe layer beneath the batting is also worth noticing: the design is about how the two layers work together, as well as the choice of surface.

3. A Shoulder Curve and a 43×63 cm Size

The edge that meets the shoulders has a gentle curve. The manufacturer describes this as a way to fill the gap between the neck and shoulders and reduce strain on the body. Claims about maintaining a natural sleeping posture and making turning easier are also the manufacturer’s descriptions.

Whether that shape suits me is a separate consideration. I would not assume everyone will experience the same comfort simply from looking at the shape.

The pillow measures 43×63 cm, and the manufacturer says it can be used with commercially available pillowcases. A cover is not included. If I planned to reuse one, I would check its supported dimensions and shape.

4. Height of Approximately 13–14 cm: Adjustability Is Unconfirmed

The published height is approximately 13–14 cm. I did not find information about height adjustment or replacement filling in the product materials checked for this review.

That does not establish that adjustment is impossible. Anyone who needs an adjustable pillow should ask the retailer before buying.

Comparing the dimensions with a current pillow can help establish its size. However, the specifications do not give its height under the weight of a head or quantify how much it compresses. I would not decide that it fits me from the 13–14 cm figure alone.

Alongside my material preferences, I would check the return and exchange conditions if I had concerns about the height.

5. Care: Hand Washing, Sun Drying, and Recommended Spot Cleaning

The manufacturer provides these care instructions and precautions:

  • Gently wash the pillow by hand and dry it in the sun.

  • Spot cleaning is recommended because drying takes time.

  • Do not use a tumble dryer or hair dryer.

  • Do not pull forcefully.

  • Keep it away from fire.

Being washable is appealing, but this is not a pillow intended for an easy washing-machine-and-dryer routine. The manufacturer specifically warns that it takes time to dry.

I would focus on keeping the cover clean and follow the spot-cleaning instructions when the pillow itself becomes dirty. Anyone who wants to wash an entire pillow frequently should consider that effort before choosing it.

6. Made in Japan, Based on a Pillow Supplied to Hotels

The press release says the product was redesigned for home use from a professional-use pillow with a history of shipments to hotels.

It does not name those hotels, state how many used the original pillow, or give the period of use. I would not turn that into a claim that Docchimo Pillow itself is used by a particular hotel.

I take this as background on how the product was developed. Likewise, “made in Japan” describes manufacturing, including domestic sewing and finishing. It does not, on its own, guarantee that the pillow will suit me or establish its durability.

Robu's Take

I would put this pillow on the shortlist for someone who:

  • Wants softer and firmer surfaces in one pillow

  • Is interested in batting supported by a separate pipe layer

  • Wants a 43×63 cm pillow to use with a compatible cover

  • Prefers a product sewn and finished in Japan

Anyone who needs height adjustment or frequent, convenient full washing should check the specifications and care requirements first. The materials reviewed here also do not establish a basis for choosing this pillow to improve shoulder or neck symptoms.

My three checks before buying would be:

  • Consider whether the approximately 13–14 cm height and materials suit my preferences, and ask about adjustability if needed.

  • Check the product name, price, and availability when ordering.

  • Confirm the return and exchange conditions that apply to this particular product.

Turning one pillow over to choose tonight’s feel is an appealing idea. Both the announced price and the sales price retrieved during this review are ¥5,500 including tax. I would make my decision after checking the height and return conditions, rather than on price alone.
