A Stop at Kainan Sakuas: Fig Grand Parfait, Bluefin Tuna and Mikan

On October 11, 2026, I stopped at the Kainan Sakuas roadside station while travelling toward Susami in Wakayama. The fig grand parfait I came for was excellent, while the bluefin tuna preparation and sales and the mikan section where visitors could taste before choosing made the stop especially memorable.

Kainan Sakuas, a stop along the way on October 11, 2026

A Stop on the Way Toward Susami

Kainan Sakuas is a roadside station with local farm and seafood products, dining options and a fruit parlour. My main reason for stopping was the seasonal fig grand parfait.

According to the official website, SHIMOTSU FRUITS is the on-site fruit parlour, serving items made with local fruit and milk from Kurosawa Farm.

The Fig Grand Parfait Was Worth the Stop

The grand parfait was lavishly filled with figs. It contained figs, soft serve made with milk from Kurosawa Farm, yoghurt ice cream, burnt-butter salted-caramel ice cream, black tea jelly, fig jam, a fig meringue element and whipped yoghurt.

I first thought one of the scoops was vanilla, but it was actually yoghurt ice cream. The generous figs, cool and creamy layers, fragrant salted caramel and black tea jelly worked beautifully together. It was genuinely delicious and exactly the treat I had hoped for.

Watching Bluefin Tuna Being Prepared and Sold

Another highlight was seeing bluefin tuna being prepared and sold. Watching the work up close and then choosing from the products made the shopping experience more engaging.

Bluefin tuna preparation and sales

Among the places I visited on this trip, this was where bluefin tuna felt the least expensive. That is my personal comparison from this particular journey; prices and products change from day to day.

The bluefin tuna show schedule displayed on the day of my visit

The Ministry of Land, Infrastructure, Transport and Tourism describes the bluefin tuna dismantling show as being planned almost daily, depending on supply. Times and availability can change, so the latest official notice should be checked on the day.

Taste Mikan Before Choosing Your Favourite

The wide variety of mikan was another excellent feature. Visitors could taste samples and choose the ones they preferred. Of the places I stopped on this trip, the mikan selection here felt the most extensive.

A mikan section where visitors can taste before choosing

The produce section filled with mikan

The ministry's roadside-station guide says that a seasonal “Mikan Road” is set up roughly from October through March, with a tasting corner for comparing fruit from different growers. Details may vary with the season, so check current information before visiting.

Robu's Wrap-Up

Although this was only a stop on the way toward Susami, the fig grand parfait, lavishly filled with figs, was genuinely delicious and completely satisfying. I also enjoyed the bluefin tuna preparation and sales and the chance to taste different mikan.

Kainan Sakuas brings together the pleasures of eating, watching and choosing in one place. Since its fruit and product selection changes through the year, the seasonal discoveries are part of the reason to stop.
