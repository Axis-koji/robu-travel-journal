AI in ordinary-looking eyewear: could Meta Glasses become a travel companion?

Not glasses that look obviously like a gadget, but ordinary-looking frames that take photographs, play music and let you speak to AI. If I were using them while travelling, that is the kind of natural experience I would want.

These are not AR glasses

As described in the Japanese article, Meta Glasses, announced by Meta and EssilorLuxottica on June 23, 2026, combine a camera, open-ear speakers, microphones and Meta AI without a screen. They are neither display-based AR glasses nor retinal-projection glasses.

Prices from US$299

The announcement describes 26 styles, Standard and Large frames, and support for prescription lenses. Battery life is stated as more than eight hours, with up to 40 hours of additional power from the charging case. The new Muse Spark AI model is scheduled to roll out first in the United States and Canada, followed by other markets.

Useful for travel—but be considerate with the camera

Being able to photograph the view in front of you is convenient. However, even though a white LED on the front flashes during recording, obtain consent when photographing people. Follow the rules of museums, healthcare facilities, schools and restricted areas.

Can you buy them in Japan?

A Japanese launch of Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 2) and Oakley Meta was announced on May 21, 2026. However, the Japanese launch, official price, supported features, warranty and repair contacts for this new series had not been confirmed in the original article. Simply converting US$299 into yen does not establish its Japanese price.

Note: Pedestrian navigation is a planned future feature. Software updates may change functions, languages and availability by region. Before use, check how the camera, microphone, location data and voice information are handled.
