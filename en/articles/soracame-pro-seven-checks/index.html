Is Soracame Pro a Good Fit? Seven Checks on LTE, IP65, and 4x Zoom

Soracom began selling Soracame Pro on October 28, 2026. It is an outdoor cloud camera with built-in LTE connectivity. I have not installed the device or tested its image quality and connection stability. This assessment is based on Soracom's announcement, product page, service information, and terms. I will keep the official specifications separate from my own purchasing judgment.

The short version is that this is not a home-security camera for individual consumers. It is aimed at business sites that have power, cannot easily add Wi-Fi, and need regular remote observation.

1. Business customers only, with hardware and a monthly service

Only corporate customers can buy Soracame Pro. The price is open, so the hardware price and monthly fee must be requested from Soracom.

The arrangement combines a purchased camera with a monthly cloud-recording and dedicated-SIM contract. The hardware and service cannot be contracted separately. Soracom does not offer a lease or rental plan.

That means a company cannot simply rent one unit as a low-commitment trial. A useful quotation should show both the hardware cost and the recurring service cost before a team compares it with alternatives.

2. LTE removes the Wi-Fi job, not the power and coverage checks

The camera uses LTE on NTT Docomo's network. It does not need a Wi-Fi installation, but it does need stable AC 100 V power. Soracom says unstable portable generators are unsuitable.

Soracom also requires a network diagnosis at the intended site. Available bandwidth can prove insufficient after installation, and the company discourages installation in high places where troubleshooting would be difficult.

In my view, coverage validation comes before enthusiasm about the camera specifications. A strong feature list cannot compensate for an unreliable connection at the actual mounting point.

3. Recording retention and export costs need careful reading

Recording-retention plans cover 7, 14, 30, 60, or 90 days. A new purchase starts on the seven-day plan. There is no minimum service period, billing is monthly, and fees are not prorated. A lower-cost suspended state is available, but the exact amount is not shown on the official product page and is therefore not stated here.

Each camera includes 72 hours per month of data-export availability, and recorded playback counts toward that allowance. Usage above the limit costs ¥66 per hour. Live viewing does not count toward the 72-hour allowance.

The limit deserves attention if a team expects to review long stretches of recordings every day. An operation focused mainly on occasional live checks may be less affected.

4. Outdoor camera specifications

The main published specifications are:

  • 720p HD video, up to 4x optical zoom, and a diagonal field of view from 123.4° to 27.9°
  • motion detection and infrared night vision up to nine metres, depending on conditions
  • IP65 protection and an operating range from -20°C to 60°C
  • approximately 250 g and 64.0 × 67.5 × 75.0 mm

The package includes a waterproof power adapter, a dedicated clamp mount, and a dedicated SIM. The device is not designed for salt-damage environments.

The 720p resolution points to situational awareness rather than fine forensic detail. The 4x optical zoom may help an operator inspect a more distant part of the scene without relying only on digital enlargement.

5. Encryption specifications do not replace operating rules

Soracom lists end-to-end encryption, HTTPS for cloud recording, and DTLS for live video.

Those specifications do not automatically make the entire operation safe. A customer still needs access controls, rules for who can view footage, and a privacy policy for people or neighbouring property that may enter the frame. That preparation is especially important at construction sites and managed properties where many people may pass through.

6. Warranty and cancellation affect footage planning

The one-year warranty applies only when Soracom confirms a natural failure. Advance replacement service is not offered.

Cancellation requires special care: access to recorded video ends immediately, and the cancellation cannot be reversed. The loaned SIM must be returned at the customer's expense.

A team should therefore export any footage it must retain before cancelling or replacing a unit. The procedure should be part of the operating plan, not an afterthought on the day the contract ends.

7. AI analysis requires Soracom Flux

When Soracame Pro is combined with Soracom Flux, extracted images can feed generative-AI workflows for object detection, reading numbers or text, recording results, and sending notifications. Reading a gauge and recording the value is one possible workflow.

The AI capability is not automatically included in the camera itself. Flux is a separate service and may add separate charges, so its current terms and pricing need to be included in the quotation.

Soracom identifies construction sites, factories and plants, river monitoring, and property management as use cases. These are places where an on-site visit is costly but periodic visual confirmation still matters.

Robu's Take

Soracame Pro looks most suitable for a corporate site with stable AC 100 V power, difficult Wi-Fi installation, and a recurring need for remote observation. Construction, industrial facilities, rivers, and managed property fit that pattern.

I would pause if power is unstable, the proposed mounting point is hard to reach, or the total cost is still unclear. Because pricing is open and Soracom offers neither rental nor leasing, it is better to obtain a complete quotation before deciding.

Three checks should come first:

  1. Connectivity: complete the network diagnosis at the exact site and confirm adequate bandwidth.
  2. Total cost and retention: compare the hardware, monthly fee, retention period, 72-hour export allowance, and ¥66 hourly overage.
  3. Installation, access, and footage preservation: decide who can view video, how privacy will be handled, and how required recordings will be saved before cancellation.

If those three points are settled, Soracame Pro could be a serious candidate for reducing routine trips made only to see what is happening on site.
